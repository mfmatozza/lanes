## What this shows, honestly
- With team context on, every I1 and I3 run passed, at no cost in turns and with no question to the user. The read heads-up fired in every I1 run (2 per run).
- The control did not fail often enough for the difference to be large: 20 of 20 against 17 of 20. That is consistent with team context fixing the stale-caller case, and it is a small sample where the case arose in only about a third of the control runs. It is not proof.
- The I2 D2 miss (9 of 10) was a behaviour failure of the renaming agent, not a collision; the stream is in the run folder.
