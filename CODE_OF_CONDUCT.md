# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be welcoming and respectful, give and take feedback with care, and keep harassment and personal attacks out. Maintainers may remove comments, commits and contributions that do not follow it, and may ban people who repeatedly break it.

To report a problem, contact the maintainers via a GitHub issue or security advisory. Use a [private security advisory](https://github.com/mfmatozza/lanes/security/advisories/new) for anything you do not want public. Reports are handled confidentially.
